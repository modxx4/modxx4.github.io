MODXX4 Afterglow host integration

https://github.com/X-F1REBALL-X/ps4-jb

In ps4_offsets.js, firmware 14.00 and 14.02 have separate firmware entries but share the Afterglow chain, payload.bin and patches/1400.bin.

Imported into MODXX4 for integration. This import alone does not certify successful operation on physical PS4 hardware; test before enabling the home-page card.
