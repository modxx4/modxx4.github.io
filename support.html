Need Help?

Quick fixes for WebKit errors, cache issues, and direct links to contact MODXX4 support.

QUICK FIXESCOMMON PS4 / PS5 SOLUTIONS
1. Memory Full Error

Press OK, wait 5 seconds, or close extra browser tabs and try again.

2. Refresh Offline Cache

Browser Options > Settings > Delete Cookies & Clear Website Data.

3. Kernel Panic / Shutdown

Wait 30 seconds after PS4 boots up before opening the browser.
